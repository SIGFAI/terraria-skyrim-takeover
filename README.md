# Skyrim Takeover

**Terraria** × Skyrim · visual · idea by the team

Bring Skyrim into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> A dragon torched my wooden town while draugr got yeeted into orbit by FUS RO DAH. Turns out Whiterun guards still have arrow-to-the-knee stories.

## Install

Copy Sigf.tmod into Documents/My Games/Terraria/tModLoader/Mods and enable it in tModLoader's Workshop > Manage Mods menu. Start a world: Whiterun appears at spawn and the Dragonbone Blade is your weapon.
